# Privacy Policy for TubeTrim

**Last updated:** September 2026

TubeTrim ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how TubeTrim handles user information.

---

### 1. No Data Collection or Tracking

- TubeTrim **does not collect, store, transmit, or sell** any personal information, browsing history, or user data.
- The extension does not use any tracking pixels, analytics services, cookies, or remote telemetries.
- All code runs 100% client-side inside your browser.

---

### 2. Permissions Justification

TubeTrim requests only the absolute minimum permissions required to perform its function:

- **`storage`**: Used solely to save your local UI preferences (such as whether Shorts or Playables are hidden) inside your browser's `chrome.storage.local`. This data never leaves your device.
- **`*://*.youtube.com/*` (Host Permissions)**: Required strictly to inject the local content script and CSS stylesheets onto YouTube pages to hide distraction elements and redirect Shorts links to the standard player.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

- TubeTrim makes **zero external network requests**.
- The extension contains no third-party scripts, libraries, or CDNs.

---

### 4. Changes to This Policy

If changes are made to this policy, the updated policy will be posted within the repository and release notes.

---

### 5. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
